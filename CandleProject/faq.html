FAQ

Our candles are made with high-quality soy wax, cotton wicks, and carefully selected fragrance oils. Soy wax burns cleaner and longer than traditional paraffin wax.

Yes! Our candles are free from parabens, phthalates, and harsh chemicals. We strive to use eco-conscious materials and sustainable packaging whenever possible.

Burn time depends on the size of the candle.
  • Small candles: approximately 20-25 hours
  • Medium candles: approximately 40-45 hours
  • Large candles: approximately 60+ hours
Always follow candle care instructions for best results.

For the best burn:
  • Trim the wick to ¼ inch before each use
  • Allow the wax to melt all the way to the edges on the first burn
  • Do not burn for more than 4 hours at a time
This helps prevent tunneling and extends the life of your candle. Additional candle care information can be found here
