About Everglow

Everglow began with a simple idea: to bring warmth, light, and a touch of magic into every home. Our founder, inspired by the cozy glow of candlelight on quiet evenings, started crafting candles in a small kitchen with just a few essential oils, natural waxes, and a deep love for hand-poured quality. What started as a way to unwind and create something meaningful soon became a passion—one rooted in slowing down, savoring the moment, and appreciating the beauty found in simple rituals.

Over the years, what began as a personal hobby gently blossomed into a full-fledged shop, built on creativity, care, and connection. Each Everglow candle is thoughtfully designed to do more than illuminate a room—it tells a story. From calming lavender fields to warm vanilla evenings and crisp seasonal blends, our scents are inspired by real moments, memories, and moods. Every candle is carefully hand-poured in small batches using premium ingredients, ensuring a clean burn, long-lasting fragrance, and a product we are truly proud to share.

As Everglow grew, so did our commitment to craftsmanship and sustainability. We believe in creating products that are as kind to the environment as they are to your home. That means responsibly sourced materials, thoughtful packaging, and a focus on quality over quantity. No shortcuts—just candles made with intention, patience, and a touch of creativity in every detail.

From our humble beginnings to becoming a favorite in homes near and far, our mission remains the same: to make your space feel a little warmer, a little cozier, and a little more magical with every light. Whether you're setting the mood for a quiet night in, celebrating a special moment, or gifting someone you love, Everglow is honored to be part of your everyday rituals and meaningful moments.
